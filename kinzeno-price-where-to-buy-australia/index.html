Subscription and cancellation

Read the order summary carefully. A repeat monthly delivery is a commitment, and the seller says it can be cancelled at any time by contacting support. If you do not want a repeat order, choose the single pack if one is offered, or set a reminder to cancel before the next charge. Keep the confirmation email, which should contain the cancellation details. The ACCC publishes guidance on subscription traps, and its Scamwatch site reports on online shopping problems.

Returns, refunds and your rights

The seller states a 60-day return policy. You must first contact support to get a return authorisation, return the goods at your own cost, and the refund is issued within 14 days of the seller receiving and inspecting them. Original shipping is not refunded.

Separately, under the Australian Consumer Law, goods must be of acceptable quality and match their description, and you have remedies for faults regardless of a seller's own policy. Change-of-mind returns depend on the seller's policy. Because the seller is based in Lithuania, resolving a dispute may take longer, so paying by credit card gives you the option of asking your bank to investigate a problem.

Current prices, pack sizes and delivery options are shown on the seller's own page and may change. Check the total in your currency before you pay.

Is it worth it?

Only you can decide, but weigh the cost against what is known. We found no trials of the product, the evidence for its ingredients on the skin is limited, and there are lower-cost, better-studied things worth trying first, such as a physio-guided exercise plan. Our guide to what helps joint pain sets out the options. If you want to compare gels and supplements, read topical gels vs joint supplements.

How to compare the total cost

The headline price is only part of the cost. Before you pay, write down these figures for each option you are considering: the price per pack, the number of packs, shipping, the currency, whether tax or customs fees may apply on delivery, and the price of any repeat order. If a monthly plan is cheaper per pack, calculate what you would spend over three months if you forgot to cancel.

It also helps to compare against a realistic alternative. A physiotherapy consultation, a pharmacist consultation and a basic exercise program may cost less over a few months than repeat orders of any gel, and they address the cause of the pain rather than the sensation of it. Some people qualify for Medicare-supported chronic disease management plans or private health rebates for physiotherapy, so ask your GP what is available to you.

Finally, keep your own records. Save the order confirmation, the page showing the terms on the day you ordered, and the date by which a return must be requested, so that you can use your rights without a fight.
